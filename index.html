Google Workspace, the usual way

This is how most teams use GoogleGogle.

Emails in, emails out. A shared folder called “New Folder (2)”. A spreadsheet someone updates on Fridays, if they remember. It works. Mostly.

Gmail, Drive, Sheets, Docs, Forms and Calendar. Paid for every month, used at walking pace.

A normal day

A normal day on Google, done by hand.

  1. By hand

    9:00 Gmail

    Open the inbox. 47 unread. Star the important ones. Forget why you starred them.

  2. By hand

    10:30 Drive

    Search for “invoice final FINAL”. Find three. Pick one and hope.

  3. By hand

    11:15 Sheets

    Type yesterday’s orders in, one row at a time. Colour the late ones red.

  4. By hand

    14:00 Forms

    Open the responses tab. Copy each answer into the tracker. One. By. One.

  5. By hand

    15:30 Docs

    Make a copy of last month’s quote. Change the client’s name. Miss one.

  6. By hand

    17:45 Calendar

    Message tomorrow’s appointments to remind them. From your own phone. After hours.

Repeat tomorrow. And the day after that.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    What is Google Apps Script?

    It’s Google’s own scripting engine, built into Sheets, Docs, Gmail, Drive, Forms and Calendar. It lets those apps do things on their own: send emails, create files, move data around, and run on a schedule. It comes with every Google account at no extra cost.

    If it’s that powerful, why haven’t I heard of it?

    Because it hides behind Extensions → Apps Script, and it needs code. Most teams never open that menu. That’s exactly the gap I fill: you get the power, without having to write a line.

    Do I need to buy new software?

    No. Everything is built on Google tools you already use: Gmail, Drive, Docs, Forms, Calendar and Sheets.

    Do I need a paid Google Workspace account?

    Not always. Many things work on a free Google account. Some features, such as shared team drives, need a paid Workspace plan. I’ll check what you have during the audit and tell you honestly if an upgrade is worth it.

    Is my data safe?

    Your data stays in your own Google account. I only get the access the project needs, you decide who can see what, and you can remove my access at any time. I don’t copy your data anywhere else.

    Can you build or redesign my website?

    Yes. I design websites, and I can connect a new or existing site to a lead tracker and automatic replies.

    What if we outgrow it?

    Google tools have limits, and I’ll be honest about them. If your business grows past what they can handle, our build department can make you a proper custom web app. That’s the orange bubble.

    How long does a project take?

    Most single-process projects take about two weeks. Bigger projects are scoped and priced upfront, so you know the timeline before we start.

    What does the care plan cover?

    Fixes, small changes and help when something breaks, for your Google setup and your website. It’s optional, and you can stop anytime.
