Case study · Clinic

From WhatsApp bookings to a calendar the whole team can manage

Client
A clinic in Malaysia
What I built
Online appointment booking, a no-show blocklist, a shared calendar manager and automatic reminder emails
Built with
Google Calendar, Google Sheets, Google Forms and Apps Script

The problem

Appointments were booked entirely through WhatsApp. Every booking meant a back-and-forth chat, and someone on the team had to check availability and enter it by hand.

Step 1: Move bookings to Google Calendar

I set up Google Calendar as the single place for appointments. Patients could now book a slot themselves instead of messaging the team, and staff could see the whole schedule in one view.

Step 2: Make blocking and releasing dates easy

Booking online created a new chore. The team had to keep blocking and releasing dates for leave, public holidays and fully booked days, and doing it directly in Google Calendar was slow and tedious. The business also wanted more control over who could book.

So I built a simple calendar manager, connected to Google Calendar through Apps Script. Anyone on the team can block or release dates in a few clicks, without needing to know how Google Calendar works behind the scenes.

Step 3: Stop repeat no-shows

Some people booked multiple appointments and didn’t turn up. I added a blocklist in a spreadsheet. When the team adds a phone number or email address to the list, that person can no longer make bookings. The clinic stays in control, and the rule is applied the same way every time.

The tool is designed around the people who use it every day. Staff don’t have to learn Google Calendar’s inner workings, they just use a simple interface. The automation handles the repetitive parts: confirmations, reminders and the blocklist.
